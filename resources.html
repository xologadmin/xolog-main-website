Downloadable checklist

Prepare an accurate quote request

Include the details below so the operations team can compare viable routes without avoidable follow-up.

  • Origin and final destination
  • Commodity and HS code, if known
  • Package count, dimensions and gross weight
  • Preferred mode and required delivery date
  • Incoterm and customs requirements
  • Hazardous, temperature or special handling needs
FAQ

International freight questions

Incoterms define how transport costs, tasks and risk are divided between seller and buyer. The commercial agreement should identify the selected rule and named place.

Carrier liability is limited and is not cargo insurance. Discuss cargo value and risk with your broker or XOLOG contact before dispatch.

Incomplete commercial documents, inconsistent values, missing permits and inaccurate commodity classifications are frequent causes of delay.

Book as early as practical, especially during peak periods. Share your required date so XOLOG can recommend a realistic cutoff and routing.
